Last updated: September 27, 2026

Sudokuduel is designed to work without requiring an account or providing personal information.

Sudokuduel does not display advertisements and does not use Firebase Analytics or other third-party analytics services.

Because Sudokuduel does not collect personal information, we do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or analytics.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties because Sudokuduel does not collect personal information through the App.

Sudokuduel is intended to provide its game functionality without requiring users to create an account or submit personal information. If a future version adds a third-party service or feature that changes the information handled by the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Sudokuduel does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The App does not require an account or request personal information.

7. Data Security

We do not maintain a server-side database of Sudokuduel users' personal information. Any information stored locally by the App for gameplay purposes remains on the user's device unless the operating system or another service handles it separately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.

9. Contact

For privacy questions concerning Sudokuduel, please use the developer contact information provided on the Sudokuduel Google Play listing.

10. Your Consent

By using Sudokuduel, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
