Last updated: September 27, 2026

LexiFall does not display advertisements and does not use Firebase Analytics or other third-party analytics services.

Because LexiFall does not collect personal information, we do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or analytics.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties because LexiFall does not collect personal information through the App.

LexiFall is intended to provide its game functionality without requiring users to create an account or submit personal information. If a future version adds a third-party service or feature that changes the information handled by the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

We do not maintain a server-side database of LexiFall users' personal information. Any information stored locally by the App for gameplay purposes remains on the user's device unless the operating system or another service handles it separately.

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.

By using LexiFall, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
